# Privacy Policy for Pitstop ⚡

*Effective Date: September 1, 2026*

Pitstop provides vehicle fault diagnosis, roadside emergency assistance, garage discovery, and fleet maintenance tools. This policy explains how information is handled within the **Pitstop Android Application** (`com.pitstop.vehiclefix`) and Web companion.

---

## 1. Permissions & Data Collected

- **Camera & Flashlight (Optional)**: Used locally on-device for AI visual defect scanning and roadside emergency safety beacon illumination.
- **Location (Optional - Fine & Coarse)**: Used exclusively while interacting with the Garages tab or broadcasting GPS coordinates during an emergency breakdown. Location is never collected or tracked continuously in the background.
- **Vehicle Profiles & Diagnostic Metrics**: Garage vehicles, OBD-II error codes, and maintenance logs are stored in secure local application storage.
- **Emergency Contacts**: Stored locally on the user's device for rapid single-tap SOS dispatch.

## 2. Data Retention & Privacy-First Architecture

Pitstop operates with an **Offline-First** model:
- Diagnostic manual libraries and lookup tables function without requiring internet connectivity.
- User data is not sold, rented, or monetized with advertising brokers.
