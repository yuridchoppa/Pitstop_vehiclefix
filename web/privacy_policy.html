Privacy Policy for Pitstop

Effective Date: September 1, 2026 | Last Updated: September 1, 2026

Pitstop ("we", "our", or "us") provides vehicle fault diagnosis, roadside emergency assistance, garage discovery, and fleet maintenance tools. This Privacy Policy outlines how your data is collected, used, and protected when using the Pitstop Android Application and Web Companion.

1. Permissions & Data Collected

  • Camera & Flashlight (Optional): Used locally to capture vehicle damage photos for AI visual diagnosis and activate the roadside SOS safety flashlight. Camera previews are processed on-device and are not shared or stored on external servers without your permission.
  • Precise / Coarse Location (Optional): Used solely to display nearby certified garages and provide instant GPS coordinates during emergency roadside breakdowns. Location data is never tracked in the background or sold to third parties.
  • Vehicle & Diagnostic Data: Vehicle make, model, fuel type, odometer readings, and OBD-II trouble codes are stored locally on your device in secure app storage for maintenance tracking.
  • User Profile: Name, phone number, and optional emergency contact numbers are stored locally to autofill roadside rescue dispatches.

2. How We Use Information

  • To generate DIY mechanical repair walkthroughs and step-by-step guidance.
  • To calculate accurate tire pressures and service schedules.
  • To format instant emergency SMS/WhatsApp dispatch messages when you trigger the Roadside SOS beacon.

3. Data Storage & Security

Pitstop is designed with an Offline-First & Privacy-First architecture. All vehicle configurations, service histories, and preferences reside securely on your device. We implement industry-standard encryption and safety protocols to protect any local data against unauthorized access.

4. Third-Party Services & Disclaimers

When navigating to external phone dialers (e.g. Highway Helpline 1033 or Police 112) or map providers, standard telecom and third-party terms apply. Pitstop provides diagnostic estimates for informational and educational purposes; users should always observe personal safety near moving machinery or high-voltage electric vehicle powertrains.

5. Children's Privacy

Our application is intended for motorists and automotive owners aged 18 and above (or legally licensed drivers). We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors.
